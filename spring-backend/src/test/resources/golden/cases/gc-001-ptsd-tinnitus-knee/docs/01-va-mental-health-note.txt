Patient: SGT Avery Stone (synthetic)        DOB: 1985-04-12
Date of Visit: 2023-09-14                    Provider: Dr. Lin Patel, PsyD

CHIEF COMPLAINT
Veteran presents for follow-up of post-traumatic stress disorder related to a
2008 deployment to Kandahar. Reports recurrent nightmares four nights per week,
intrusive memories triggered by loud noises, and persistent hypervigilance.

HISTORY
SGT Stone served in the Army from 2005 to 2010. During a 2008 convoy mission the
vehicle ahead struck an improvised explosive device; the veteran witnessed the
death of a fellow soldier. Symptoms began within weeks of return and have
persisted. The veteran was formally diagnosed with PTSD in 2009 at the Phoenix
VA. Current GAF estimate 52, consistent with reduced reliability and
productivity at work; the veteran has changed jobs three times in two years due
to conflict with supervisors and difficulty concentrating.

MENTAL STATUS
Alert, oriented x3. Mood "on edge." Affect constricted. No suicidal ideation.
Sleep severely disrupted. Startle response markedly elevated.

ASSESSMENT
1. PTSD, chronic, moderate-to-severe. Occupational and social impairment with
   reduced reliability and productivity.
2. Tinnitus reported (see audiology note) — bilateral constant ringing.

PLAN
Continue sertraline 100 mg daily; prazosin 2 mg at bedtime for nightmares.
Referred to audiology for tinnitus evaluation. Return to clinic in 8 weeks.
