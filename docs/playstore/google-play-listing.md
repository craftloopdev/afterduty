## Release (App bundle)

- **Signed AAB**: built with the upload key (`~/.gcp/va-claim-upload.jks`; password in
  `web/android/keystore.properties`, gitignored). Upload SHA-1 `72:D9:AB:80:…:70:72` registered
  with the Firebase Android app.
- **Play App Signing**: enroll (Google manages the app signing key; we hold the upload key). **After
  the first upload, copy the Play app-signing SHA-1 from Play Console → App integrity into the
  Firebase Android app** — required so phone-auth attestation works on the Google-signed build.
- **Subscriptions**: create `pro_monthly` ($11.99) + `pro_annual` ($119.99) in Play Console →
  Monetize → Subscriptions, then link them in the RevenueCat dashboard (Play Store app) and set
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_REVENUECAT_ANDROID_KEY` (goog_…) so IAP works. Until then the app builds/runs but Pro
  purchase is disabled on Android.
